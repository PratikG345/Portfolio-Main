01 · About

From Operations to Engineering.

My path didn't start at a keyboard. It started in recruitment operations and ERP implementation — sitting with procurement teams, store managers, and HR leads, watching how invoices, GRNs, stock movements, and candidate pipelines actually flow.

That exposure changed how I write code. Before modeling a single table, I want to know who fills it, when, why, and what breaks if a field is wrong. Systems should mirror the way people work — not force them to adapt to software.

Today I build with Python, Django, and Django REST Framework, focusing on relational design, validated business rules, and APIs that hold up under real workflows.

ERP Backend System — Inventory & Procurement

A modular ERP backend modeling the full lifecycle from purchase order to stock movement, designed against real procurement operations.

Problem
Procurement and stores teams rely on spreadsheets, losing traceability between POs, GRNs, and stock.
Approach
Modular master data (Item, UOM, Category, Stock Location) with ForeignKey integrity, validated PO → GRN → stock movement pipeline.
Outcome
Scalable backend mirroring real ERP workflows, ready to plug into reporting and approval layers.
Stack
PythonDjango DRFPostgreSQL Data Modeling

API · Production

Student–Course Enrollment API

A DRF service modeling enrollment as a stateful, constraint-aware relationship — not just a join table.

Problem
Naive M2M enrollments allow duplicates, ignore prerequisites, and lose history.
Approach
Intermediate model with unique constraints, ACTIVE/DROPPED state machine, validated transitions.
Outcome
Production deployment on Render with predictable, idempotent enrollment workflows.
Stack
Django RESTPostgreSQLRender

LogMate — Internal Logging System

A structured daily log to replace scattered notes — built, deployed, and actively used with 60+ real entries.

Problem

Approach
CRUD workflow with time-window filters (1/7/30 days), relational schema, deploy-and-iterate cycle.
Outcome
60+ entries in production use, faster weekly reviews, clearer activity trail.
